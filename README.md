## 🔬 Research

I apply machine learning to multivariate problems in the geological and planetary sciences. I focus on how geochemical and mineralogical data are represented (compositional data transformations, feature engineering, dimensionality reduction) and how those choices affect model performance and interpretability.

**Current projects**
- **Magnetite trace-element chemistry** for iron ore deposit-type discrimination
- **Global mantle geochemistry** characterized through multiple ML methods
- **Unit-cell → mineral chemistry** prediction for planetary XRD (CheMin / Curiosity, Gale crater)
- **Zircon REE classification** of metamorphic vs. igneous origin
- **Petrographic AI/ML foundation models** and thin-section digitization (Ward's ARC collection)

Working thesis: *Multi-modal data-driven mineralogy across P-T-X conditions* (Advisor: Dr. Shaunna M. Morrison)

## 🗄️ GeoCORK

**GeoCORK** is a desktop application and workflow for managing, storing, and querying U-Pb geochronology data in a SQL database. It began as my undergraduate thesis at CSU Fullerton with Dr. Kathryn Metcalf and now includes integration pathways with EarthChem and SESAR2 (IGSN sample import).

- 📄 Metcalf, K. & **Burges, J.M.** (2026). *GeoCORK: An improved workflow for U-Pb geochronology data management.* Applied Computing and Geosciences. [doi:10.1016/j.acags.2026.100364](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acags.2026.100364)
- Presented at GSA, AGU, and Lamont-Doherty's Geoinformatics Research Group lecture series
